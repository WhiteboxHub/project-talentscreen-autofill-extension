# Privacy Policy for TalentScreen Autofill Extension

**Last Updated**: May 16, 2026

## Overview

TalentScreen Autofill Extension ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide
The Extension collects and stores the following information that you voluntarily provide:

- **Resume Data**: Your personal information in JSON format including:
  - Name, email, phone number, address
  - Work history, education, skills
  - Professional summary and career information
  - Custom fields (EEO information, work authorization, etc.)

- **Resume Files**: PDF or DOC files containing your resume

- **Application Tracking**: 
  - URLs of job application pages visited
  - Form completion timestamps
  - Application status (completed, in progress)

### Automatically Collected Data
- **Extension Usage**: Form detection events, autofill success rates (stored locally for debugging)

## How We Store Your Data

**All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage API.**

- ✅ Data **never leaves your device**
- ✅ No cloud storage or external servers
- ✅ No data transmission to third parties
- ✅ No account creation required
- ✅ You have complete control over your data

## How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for the following purposes:

1. **Autofilling Job Applications**: To automatically fill form fields on job application websites
2. **Application Tracking**: To help you track your job applications
3. **Settings Management**: To display and edit your resume information within the extension

## Data Sharing

**We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit your data to any third parties.**

Your data remains on your device at all times. The Extension does not:
- Upload data to external servers
- Share data with advertisers
- Transmit data to analytics services
- Send data to the extension developer

## Permissions Explained

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

### storage
**Purpose**: Store your resume data and application tracking locally on your device  
**Data Access**: Local device storage only

### activeTab
**Purpose**: Detect job application forms on the page you're currently viewing  
**Data Access**: Current tab content only when extension is active

### tabs
**Purpose**: Automatically open the extension when you visit supported job sites  
**Data Access**: URL of current tab (no page content)

### sidePanel
**Purpose**: Display the extension interface  
**Data Access**: None

### contextMenus
**Purpose**: Provide right-click menu options for quick access  
**Data Access**: None

### Host Permissions (26+ ATS domains)
**Purpose**: Access job application forms on supported ATS platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, LinkedIn, SmartRecruiters, etc.)  
**Data Access**: Form fields on these specific domains only

## Data Retention

Your data is retained in Chrome's local storage until you:
- Manually delete it through the extension settings
- Uninstall the extension
- Clear Chrome's extension data

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: View all your stored data through the extension settings
- **Modify**: Edit your resume data anytime
- **Delete**: Remove all data by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage
- **Export**: Download your data (JSON format available in settings)

## Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

- All data stored locally using Chrome's secure storage API
- No external data transmission
- No backend servers or databases
- No authentication tokens or credentials stored

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Third-Party Services

The Extension does **not** use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

The Extension **does** interact with job application websites (ATS platforms) to fill forms, but does not transmit your data to these sites beyond what you would manually enter.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Posting the new policy in the Chrome Web Store listing
- (For significant changes) Displaying a notification in the extension
