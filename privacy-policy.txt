PRIVACY POLICY
TalentScreen Autofill Extension
Last Updated: May 16, 2026

=============================================================================

QUICK SUMMARY

TalentScreen Autofill Extension stores all your data locally on your device. 
We never transmit, upload, or share your information with anyone. Your resume 
data never leaves your computer.

=============================================================================

1. OVERVIEW

TalentScreen Autofill Extension ("the Extension") is committed to protecting 
your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard 
your information when you use our Chrome extension.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Data You Provide:
- Resume Data (JSON format): Name, email, phone, address, work history, 
  education, skills, professional summary
- Resume Files: PDF or DOC files
- Custom Fields: EEO information, work authorization, technical screening 
  answers, application preferences
- Application Tracking: URLs, timestamps, status

2.2 Automatically Collected Data:
- Extension Usage: Form detection events, autofill success rates (local only)

3. HOW WE STORE YOUR DATA

ALL DATA IS STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE USING CHROME'S LOCAL STORAGE API.

✓ Data NEVER leaves your device
✓ No cloud storage or external servers
✓ No data transmission to third parties
✓ No account creation required
✓ You have complete control over your data

4. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your data is used solely for:
- Autofilling job applications
- Application tracking
- Settings management

5. DATA SHARING

WE DO NOT SHARE, SELL, RENT, OR TRANSMIT YOUR DATA TO ANY THIRD PARTIES.

The Extension does NOT:
- Upload data to external servers
- Share data with advertisers
- Transmit data to analytics services
- Send data to the extension developer
- Use tracking pixels or cookies

6. CHROME PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

storage: Store resume data locally on your device
activeTab: Detect forms on current page
tabs: Auto-open extension on job sites
sidePanel: Display extension interface
contextMenus: Right-click menu access
Host Permissions: Access 26+ ATS domains for form filling

7. DATA RETENTION

Your data is retained until you:
- Manually delete it through extension settings
- Uninstall the extension
- Clear Chrome's extension data

8. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:
- Access: View all stored data in settings
- Modify: Edit your resume data anytime
- Delete: Remove all data by uninstalling
- Export: Download your data (JSON)

9. DATA SECURITY

Security measures:
- Local storage using Chrome's secure API
- No external transmission
- No backend servers
- No stored credentials

10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information 
from children.

11. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Extension does NOT use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking 
tools.

12. COMPLIANCE

Complies with:
- Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

For EU Users (GDPR):
You have full control to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object 
to processing through extension settings.

For California Users (CCPA):
You have the right to know, delete, and opt-out. Note: We do not sell data.
